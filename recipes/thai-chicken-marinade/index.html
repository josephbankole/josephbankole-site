Thai Chicken Marinade

Chicken thighs in a cilantro root and garlic paste with soy, fish sauce, honey, turmeric and chilli flakes, marinated for at least three hours and then baked. The cilantro roots are the part most shortcut recipes drop, and they are what makes this taste like gai yang rather than generic sweet soy chicken. The notebook page carries ingredients and nothing else, so the method here is drawn from published versions of the same paste. Reckon on ten minutes of work, a long wait in the fridge, and fifty minutes in the oven. Chicken is done at 74C / 165F in the thickest part.

Joseph Bankole30 August 20262 min read

Before you cook this.

Chicken is done at 74C / 165F in the thickest part. Turmeric stains the meat yellow all the way through, so colour is not a doneness cue on this one and the thermometer is doing all the work. Thighs are forgiving above that number, so err high rather than low.

The used marinade has held raw chicken for hours. Do not spoon it over the cooked meat. The tray juices are a different thing and are fine to baste with, because they have been in the oven the whole time. If you want the marinade itself as a sauce, boil it hard for a couple of minutes first.

Marinate in the fridge and never on the counter, over a window this long. Turmeric also stains hands, worktops and cloth permanently, so it is worth wearing gloves and keeping it off anything porous.

Ingredients

  • 2-4 chicken thighs
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon of turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon of fish sauce
  • 2 teaspoons of honey
  • 1 teaspoon of chilli flakes
  • Cilantro roots

Equipment. Pestle and mortar, Instant-read thermometer

Method

  1. Rinse the cilantro roots and scrape them clean
  2. Pound the cilantro roots and garlic to a rough paste
  3. Stir in the soy sauce, fish sauce, honey, turmeric and chilli flakes
  4. Score the chicken thighs on the skin side
  5. Coat the thighs in the marinade
  6. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours, overnight is better
  7. Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking
  8. Bake skin side up at 350F for 40 minutes, basting with the tray juices
  9. Raise the heat to 390F for a final 10 minutes to caramelise
  10. Rest for 3 minutes before serving

How long does it need to marinate?

Three hours as a minimum, overnight for preference, and no more than 24 hours. The page itself gives no marinating time, which for a recipe whose subject is a marinade is the gap that matters, so this figure comes from published versions rather than from him.

Take the thighs out of the fridge half an hour before they go in the oven. Cold meat straight into a hot oven cooks unevenly and the outside overshoots while the middle catches up.

Where the method came from

Published gai yang and southern Thai turmeric chicken recipes, both built on the same cilantro root and garlic base, agreed closely enough on sequence that the steps transfer without substitution. Only the sweetener varies between them, and honey is already on his list.

Watch the last ten minutes. Honey caramelises fast and 390F will take a glaze from sticky to burnt quicker than the clock suggests, so use your eyes for that stretch rather than the timer.
