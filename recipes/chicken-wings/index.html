Chicken Wings

Two kilos of chicken wings marinated in light and dark soy, oyster sauce and cooking wine. Dredge them in flour and corn starch, then fry them twice. Savoury and salty rather than hot, with a thin brittle crust off the corn starch. Allow about 50 minutes. Check the thickest wing reads at least 74C / 165F.

Joseph Bankole30 August 20261 min read

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Makes 6 to 8

Ingredients

  • 2 kg of chicken wings
  • 1/2 cup of flour
  • 1/2 cup of corn starch
  • 1.5 teaspoons of salt
  • 3 teaspoons of sugar
  • 1.5 teaspoons of white pepper
  • 3 tablespoons of light soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons of oyster sauce
  • 3 tablespoons of cooking wine
  • 1.5 tablespoons of dark soy sauce
  • Neutral oil for deep frying (not in the original notebook, steps 8 and 9 fry the wings at a stated temperature but no oil appears in his list)

Equipment. A deep, heavy pot or a fryer, Instant-read thermometer, Wire rack

Method

  1. Wash and cut wings
  2. Mix together marinade
  3. Marinate chicken
  4. In a bowl mix flour, corn starch and seasoning
  5. Pat dry the chicken wings
  6. Roll the wings in flour to coat
  7. Squeeze the wing to incorporate
  8. Fry at 350F for about 7 minutes, in batches of roughly 1.5 lbs so the oil holds its temperature
  9. Rest the wings for 5 minutes
  10. Fry again at 375F for about 7 minutes
  11. Check the thickest wing reads at least 74C / 165F before serving. 79C / 175F eats better on a wing

How long do they need in the oil?

About fourteen minutes in total, split across two fries with a rest between. That is 350F for around seven minutes, five minutes off the heat, then 375F for around seven more.

Use the thermometer, not the clock. Two kilos is a lot for a domestic pot. Work in batches of about 1.5 lbs and let the oil climb back between them.
