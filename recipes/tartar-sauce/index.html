Tartar Sauce

A cup of mayonnaise stirred through with finely diced dill pickles and shallot. Chopped capers, a tablespoon of pickle juice and the juice and zest of a whole lemon go in too. Ten minutes of chopping, then an hour in the fridge. It makes about a cup and a half, and it keeps up to two weeks in a sealed container.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Makes About 1.5 cups, 8 to 12 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of mayonnaise
  • 2 dill pickles, finely diced
  • 1 shallot, finely diced
  • 1 tablespoon of capers, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon of pickle juice
  • 1 lemon (juice and zest)

Equipment. Zester or fine grater, Airtight container

Method

  1. Combine the ingredients for the tartar sauce
  2. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, an hour is better. Taste and season after the rest, since the salt reads differently once it settles

Does it need to rest?

Yes. At least 30 minutes, and an hour is better.

Season after the rest rather than before. Pickles, capers and pickle juice all carry salt, and how much of it reads changes once everything has sat together.

What do you serve it with?

Beer battered fish. Make the sauce first so it can rest while the fish is cut, salted and battered.

It keeps up to two weeks in an airtight container in the fridge. Keep it cold at the table rather than letting the bowl warm next to hot fish.
