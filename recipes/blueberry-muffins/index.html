Blueberry Muffins

Creamed-butter blueberry muffins with a full cup of plain yogurt, which keeps them soft into a second day. Cream the butter and sugar, add the eggs, then the wet ingredients, then fold in the dry ingredients and the frozen berries. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes at 350F. About twenty minutes of work.

Joseph Bankole30 August 20261 min read

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Makes about 16 muffins

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups of butter (room temp)
  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 2 eggs (room temp)
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1/4 cup of milk
  • 2.5 cups of flour
  • 2 teaspoons of baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 1/2 to 1 cup of frozen blueberries

Equipment. 12-hole muffin tin, Paper liners

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 350F
  2. Cream the butter and sugar
  3. Add the eggs one at a time
  4. Add the vanilla, yogurt and milk, then mix
  5. Combine the dry ingredients in a separate bowl
  6. Combine everything with the blueberries
  7. Bake for 25-30 mins

How do you get a good crumb?

Stop mixing the moment the flour disappears. Overmixing makes a tough muffin with tunnels through it.

Let the eggs, yogurt and milk come to room temperature. Cold liquid hitting creamed butter splits the batter.

Toss the frozen blueberries in a spoonful of the measured flour before folding them in, or they sink to the bottom. Fill the liners right to the top for a domed muffin.
