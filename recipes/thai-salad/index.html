Thai Salad

Chicken thighs cooked in green seasoning, chopped small and tossed through lettuce, cilantro, shredded carrot, cucumber and roasted peanuts. Sweet chilli sauce is the dressing, so it reads sweet rather than sharp. Two thighs and a bag of salad make a main for two or a side for four. About half an hour. Cook the chicken to 74C / 165F.

Joseph Bankole30 August 20261 min read

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Makes 2 as a main, or 4 as a side

Ingredients

  • 2 seasoned chicken thighs
  • 2 servings of lettuce or salad mix
  • 1 tablespoon of chopped cilantro
  • 1/2 cup of shredded carrots
  • 1/4 of a cucumber (sliced)
  • 1/4 cup roasted peanuts
  • 1/2 cup of sweet chilli sauce
  • Green seasoning, for cooking the chicken (not in the original notebook, named in step 1 but absent from his list)

Equipment. Instant-read thermometer

Method

  1. Cook chicken with green seasoning
  2. Chop the chicken into small pieces
  3. Mix all ingredients together

Is half a cup of dressing too much?

It is generous for two servings, and all eight tablespoons are a sugar-based bottled sauce. Dress it at the last minute or the leaves slump.

To sharpen it, cut the sweet chilli to a quarter cup and add a tablespoon of lime juice with two teaspoons of fish sauce. Or leave the half cup alone and call it four servings.
