Cream Cheese Dip

A no-cook dip. Beat 500g of cream cheese smooth with the juice of a lemon, then fold through capers and 250g of smoked salmon. Chill it for an hour, then leave it out a few minutes so it scoops. Smoke, salt and lemon against a thick dairy base. Fifteen minutes of work and about four cups, enough for sixteen to twenty people.

Joseph Bankole30 August 20261 min read

  • Prep 15 min
  • Makes About 4 cups, serves 16 to 20

Ingredients

  • 1 500g pack cream cheese
  • 5 teaspoons capers
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 250 g of hot-smoked salmon

Equipment. Mixing bowl, Hand mixer or food processor

Method

  1. Bring the cream cheese to room temperature
  2. Chop the capers finely
  3. Flake or finely chop the salmon
  4. Beat the cream cheese with most of the lemon juice until smooth
  5. Fold in the salmon and capers
  6. Taste and add the remaining lemon juice a little at a time until it balances
  7. Chill for an hour before serving
  8. Let it sit out for a few minutes before serving so it scoops

Will it scoop?

Not straight from the fridge. Half a kilo of cream cheese loosened by one lemon is stiff.

Bring the cream cheese fully to room temperature before you beat it. Let the chilled dip stand a few minutes before it goes on the table.
