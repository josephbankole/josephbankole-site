Corn Bread

Corn bread on the sweet, cakey side rather than the lean savoury one, with twice as much flour as cornmeal and a full cup of sugar, so the crumb is soft instead of gritty. It uses melted butter and oil together, which is deliberate: the butter carries flavour and the oil keeps it moist for longer. Fifteen minutes to mix, then 35 to 45 minutes at 350F in a 9x13 tin. This is a double batch by most standards and serves 12 to 15.

Joseph Bankole30 August 20262 min read

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1 cup of cornmeal
  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 1.5 tablespoons of baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1/2 cup of melted butter
  • 1/2 cup of oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 1.25 cups of milk

Equipment. 9x13 inch tin or a large cast iron skillet

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 350
  2. Whisk together the dry ingredients
  3. In a separate bowl, mix together the butter, oil, milk and eggs
  4. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients
  5. Bake for 35-45 minutes

Why there are two fats

Half a cup of melted butter and half a cup of oil in the same batter looks like a transcription slip and is not. The second-ranked corn bread recipe on Google does the same thing at half the scale, quarter cup of each, for the stated reason that butter carries the flavour while oil keeps the crumb from drying out over a day or two.

Do not collapse the two into one cup of either. Butter alone goes firm and dry by the next morning; oil alone tastes of nothing much.

Is 1.5 tablespoons of baking powder right?

This is the one line I would have him check against the page. A tablespoon and a half is four and a half teaspoons, which works out at 1.5 teaspoons per cup of dry ingredients against a conventional 1 teaspoon. It is high but not impossible for a tall, cakey corn bread in a deep tin.

Tablespoon and teaspoon are the easiest pair to confuse in handwriting, and this is the dimmest photograph in the notebook. One tablespoon is the textbook figure and is the likelier intent. A teaspoon and a half would be badly under-leavened for three cups of dry, so it is not that.

Everything else on the page survived checking. I set out to break this transcription and could not.

How to get it right

Stir only until the batter comes together and a few lumps remain. Corn bread turns rubbery faster than almost any other quick bread, and overmixing is how it happens.

Three cups of dry ingredients needs a 9x13 tin or a large skillet, greased. In anything smaller the middle will not cook in 45 minutes.

Test with a skewer in the centre rather than going by the clock.
