Cookies

Chocolate chip cookies made with a cup of butter to a cup and a half of flour. A chopped chocolate bar goes in instead of chips, with a teaspoon of cornstarch for softness. Freezing the scooped dough stops it spreading flat. Twenty minutes of mixing, half an hour in the freezer, then 8 to 10 minutes at 425F. Makes about 18.

Joseph Bankole30 August 20261 min read

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Makes 18 to 20 medium cookies, or 12 large

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of butter (room temp)
  • 1/2 cup of brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup of white sugar
  • 1 egg (room temp)
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla
  • 1.5 cups of flour
  • 1 teaspoon of corn starch
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 1 bar of chocolate, 100g to 200g

Equipment. Baking sheets

Method

  1. Cream the butter and sugar
  2. Add the egg and vanilla, then mix until smooth
  3. Mix the dry ingredients in another bowl
  4. Chop the chocolate bar into small pieces
  5. Combine everything
  6. Scoop out individual cookies and freeze
  7. Bake at 425 for 8-10 mins

Why is the oven at 425F?

The dough is very high in fat and goes in frozen solid. A hot oven sets the outside before the middle has time to spread.

How do you stop the bottoms burning?

At 425F the bases set two or three minutes before the centres. Lift one cookie at 8 minutes and look underneath before you commit the tray.

Pull them while the edges are set and the middles still look wet. They finish on the hot sheet, so leave them there 5 minutes.

Freeze the scooped dough for at least 30 minutes and space the balls 2 inches apart. This much butter spreads.
