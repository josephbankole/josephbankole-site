Salon is an iPhone app that hangs one public-domain painting at a time on a museum wall.

Get Salon on the App Store

What it does

  • It learns your taste from the works you like, the ones you swipe away and how long you look at each one, and uses that to order your daily wall.
  • That record stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics code.
  • It follows your iPhone's language, in English or French.
  • It can send a daily reminder at a time you choose.
  • It has an optional soundtrack from Apple Music, which needs an Apple Music subscription.
