Salon support

Salon for iPhonejosephbankole.ca

Write to partnerships@josephbankole.ca. Tell us which iPhone you use and which version of Salon you have. You can find the version in Salon's Settings, under the gear.

Do I need an account?

No. Salon has no accounts.

How do I change the language?

Salon follows your iPhone's language, in English or French. Tap the gear, then Language, to choose a different one for Salon.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Tap the gear and use the Daily reminder setting. You can set its time there too.

What does the small printer mark mean?

A brass printer means the museum's file is large enough to print at one of the shop's sizes. A crossed-out printer means it is not.

Can I buy a print?

Not yet. The print shop isn't open. You can join the waitlist in My Shop, and we'll write to you when it opens.

Why does the soundtrack ask for Apple Music?

The optional soundtrack plays from Apple Music and needs an Apple Music subscription. Salon plays no sound of its own.

What happens to my data?

Your likes and what Salon learns from them stay on your iPhone. The privacy policy has the details.
