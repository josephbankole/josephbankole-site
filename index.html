01 / About

From bank systems to AI platforms.

I am an AI platform engineer at a global bank in Montreal, supporting enterprise deployments of Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Copilot. I came to it after ten years keeping bank systems running, the last five in payments.

I studied electrical engineering at McGill and planned on control systems, but found myself at HSBC on market risk systems instead. I spent nearly five years in Toronto supporting the platforms that run value-at-risk and Monte Carlo simulations, in a follow-the-sun team across four countries. At Société Générale in Montreal I moved to payments. I supported the SWIFT, Montran, ACH and Flexcube applications, which handle thousands of transactions a day worth millions of dollars, in a regulated correspondent bank. I led testing and deployment of the ISO 20022 MX migration across more than five business units, and it went live with zero rollbacks.

Across the two banks I shipped 134 permanent root-cause fixes and mentored five analysts. Outside the bank I run The ARCHV, a football history brand built on a production system I designed myself: over 120 Claude skills, scheduled agents for research and editing, and a native iOS app. Same discipline, different system.

I grew up in Lagos, moved to New Hampshire at nine, and came to Canada for McGill. My partner and I run our household on an app I wrote for us, and outside work I make music in Ableton Live and engineer the audio for a friend's podcast, Pros in the Arena.

I write down what I learn in the field notes, for anyone moving into this work and for anyone who wants to be happier in the job they already have. For one sourced AI story most days, follow along on Threads.
